Data access and computational scope

Expression inputs are reused from GEO GSE206285 (UNIFI), GSE212849 (PROgECT) and GSE92415 (PURSUIT). Exact byte hashes and relative paths of required inputs are recorded in DATA_INPUTS.csv. The remaining analysis inputs include curated clinical joins, frozen probe mappings, published score recipes and historical technical estimates; acceptance snapshots are identified separately by role.

The code-only candidate does not embed any required patient-level input or acceptance snapshot. Code alone, or a fresh download of a GEO matrix alone, does not reconstruct every curated input. The separately prepared complete review kit retains these exact files so that reviewer access can be provided. No author-requested new clinical data are needed for the already completed analyses.

MARS uses eleven Reactome pathways frozen at MSigDB v7.4, without KEGG, BioCarta or STKE sets in this implementation. The official MSigDB license page covers v6.0-v7.5.1 under CC BY 4.0 with specified subcollection exceptions; source acknowledgments and version identity must be retained if those inputs are redistributed. Source: https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb_license_terms.jsp
This check of MSigDB does not imply that all other third-party inputs have an identical license. Original score-paper citations and data-source identifiers remain in References_IBD.ris.
